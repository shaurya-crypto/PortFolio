About Shaurya Prabhakar

Shaurya Prabhakar is a student, builder and founder from Delhi, India, with interests spanning artificial intelligence, software development, electronics, embedded systems and creative technology.

His programming journey began at around twelve. What started as curiosity gradually became a long-term interest in understanding how technology works and, more importantly, how ideas can be turned into working systems.

His learning approach is heavily project-driven. Instead of treating programming as purely theoretical, he experiments by building applications, interfaces, hardware prototypes and technology concepts.

Over time, his interests expanded from programming and web development into microcontrollers and electronics. He has worked with platforms such as ESP32 and Raspberry Pi Pico W and experimented with sensors, audio electronics and physical computing.

Artificial intelligence has become one of his primary areas of interest. He has explored concepts including Python, NumPy, machine-learning fundamentals, transformers, embeddings and retrieval-augmented generation, while continuing to develop his understanding of modern AI systems.

His current product-building work includes StuChats, a social-learning platform concept for students, and Stratum Studios, a creative technology direction focused on premium digital experiences and cinematic 3D web experiences.

His broader goal is to keep developing the ability to move from an idea to a complete technology product — combining software, artificial intelligence, hardware and design.

Projects

StuChats

Role: Founder / Builder

StuChats is a social-learning platform concept designed around students, learning, competition, collaboration and school communities. The concept combines social interaction with learning-oriented features.

Core concepts include student communities, school-based organization, competitions, XP, leaderboards, and friends. The project involves Kotlin, Android, Supabase, and PostgreSQL.

Status: In Development

Stratum Studios

Role: Founder / Builder

Stratum Studios is a creative technology direction focused on premium digital experiences, interactive design, cinematic storytelling and 3D web experiences.

Its visual philosophy is inspired by high-quality digital products and cinematic technology experiences, utilizing Next.js, React, Three.js, React Three Fiber, GSAP, and Framer Motion.

Technical Profile & Journey

Artificial Intelligence & Software

Exploring and building with Python, NumPy, React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and Kotlin. Focused on understanding modern AI systems, neural networks, transformers, embeddings, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Electronics & Hardware

Experimenting with physical computing using ESP32, Raspberry Pi Pico W, MicroPython, sensors, servos, audio systems, and basic embedded systems.
